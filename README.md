<summary><strong>Portal Kids · What I refined on November 18, 2026</strong></summary>

- **Make the journey part of the game.** A character spirals into a translucent orb above the castle world. Tapping floating materials makes him jump to collect them and build the doorway, block by block.
- **Let kids choose the order.** Every material contributes to the next building stage, whichever one gets tapped first. I checked all six collection orders on the device.
- **Make transitions feel connected.** Purple blooms inside the finished doorway, then the next world expands from that doorway. The Creeper shortcut wiggles, unfurls a pixel ribbon and opens the next world from its own center.
- **Test the messy paths.** Automated checks cover rapid taps, interrupted collections, cancelled animations and transition positions. The final interaction was installed and checked on the family device.

</details>

## How I build

I come up with the idea and the design, and AI coding tools write all of the code—usually Claude Code, Codex, or whichever AI I feel like using that day (or still have tokens left on), with GitHub, Vercel and Figma. I iterate on the interactions, try them myself, and keep refining until they feel right.
